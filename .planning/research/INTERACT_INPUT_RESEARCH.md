# Interact input research

**Date:** 2026-10-17. Not a decision.

## What HomeWorld already locked

Encounters are one stroke you can fail. Marks do not add a verb. [`Docs/canon/PILLARS.md`](../../Docs/canon/PILLARS.md), [`Docs/canon/DESIGN_ETHOS.md`](../../Docs/canon/DESIGN_ETHOS.md).

The stroke is drawn with the cursor while the player moves. [`Docs/VIRTUE_ATTRIBUTE_BIBLE.md`](../../Docs/VIRTUE_ATTRIBUTE_BIBLE.md) Challenge, Lead 2026-10-15.

Herbs, the kettle, the spirit, and the partner are world actions. On `L_SizeTest` those already passed as direct actions. Putting the cursor on E removed that confirm.

## What a close game did

Ōkami keeps them apart. Circle is talk / investigate. Hold R1 is the Celestial Brush, and release finishes the drawing. The brush is a quasi-mode, not the button that picks things up. [GameFAQs controls](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps3/674775-okami-hd/faqs/75329/controls-general-tips), [Okami: Great Brushwork](http://gamedesignreviews.com/reviews/okami-great-brushwork/).

HomeWorld should not copy the part where the brush stops time. The locked stroke is drawn while moving.

## The split this supports

World confirm: a wisp on the object in range, then one confirm.

Rune cursor: only while that stroke is being drawn.

One button should not be both.

## Locked 2026-10-17

**Scheme:** Proximity. The wisp marks what the player is close enough to use. A confirm does the herb, the kettle, the spirit, and the partner. The cursor exists only while drawing a rune, and the player can still walk.

## Inputs locked 2026-10-17

**World confirm:** E. It uses the object the wisp has marked.

**Rune cursor:** hold the right mouse button. Only while a rune is being drawn. Walking stays on. Releasing the button leaves the cursor.

Not the press-E cursor, and not a left click to use a herb.

**Focus:** Press Q to arm the proximity mark. Press Q again to put it away. The wisp does not orbit until then. E still confirms the marked object.
