# Audio design brief (research deliverable)

**Source:** Phase 7 research (R-7-1), 2026-10-17.  
**Status:** Draft — pending Lead/art-director adoption (interview Q5).  
**Canon impact:** none yet. Would become a new `Docs/` audio bible if adopted.

## Why this exists

No audio bible is referenced anywhere in canon. Audio is a primary feel channel and is currently unspecified. This brief captures research findings and proposes a starting language.

## Day vs. night palette

- **Day (homestead):** warm, handmade, hopeful. Acoustic guitar / plucked strings, gentle ambient (birds, wind, kettle). Matches "safe home above a living world."
- **Night (spirit):** cooler, quieter, more spacious. Reverb-heavy, sparse, with a low hum. The world should sound different, not just look different.
- **Transition:** dawn/dusk as a musical phrase, not a hard cut. NightMix drives both visuals and audio.

## Spatial audio for stealth

- HomeWorld's spirit-stealth uses spirit-blue light as the danger signal ("blue = danger"). Audio should mirror this: a low hum that grows in intensity/pitch as the player enters the radius. The guard's torch and campfire each have a distinct spatial signature.
- Breaking out of the light stops the hum. This gives the player an audio channel for the stealth mechanic, supporting accessibility (Phase 6).
- Directional audio helps the player locate the patrolling guard without a HUD marker (aligns with "place teaches itself").

## Adaptive music

- Music should respond to form state: body (day) vs. spirit (night) get different layers/instrumentation.
- Spirit-sickness stacks (0–5) could modulate music (subtle detuning or added dissonance as stacks rise) — but must not become annoying. Test in Phase 1 playtest.
- Encounter state: wind-up, commit, success (yield), bonk each get distinct stings. "A verb confirms on one channel" (DESIGN_ETHOS).

## Form-change sting

- Bed → spirit: a soft, warm chime (not sci-fi). Spirit → body (dawn): a gentle exhale/resolve.
- Should feel like a breath, not a power-up.

## Bonk

- The bonk (this game's "death") should be readable but not punishing. A soft thud + brief silence, then the glide-home. Not a game-over sting.

## Nurture / care

- Nurture interactions should sound warm and satisfying (a small chime, a growth sound). Care reads as comfort (Spiritfarer principle from DESIGN_ETHOS).

## Closed captions (accessibility)

- All non-dialogue audio that carries gameplay meaning (spirit hum, bonk, wind-up, nurture success) must be captionable. See ACCESSIBILITY_CHECKLIST.md.
