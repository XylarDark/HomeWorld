# EA launch checklist (research deliverable)

**Source:** Phase 10 research (R-10-1), 2026-10-17.  
**Status:** Draft — pending Lead/business decision (interview Q6).  
**Canon impact:** none yet.

## Pre-launch (before EA)

- [ ] **Wishlist target:** 7,000+ wishlists before launch (GameDiscoverCo threshold for algorithm support).
- [ ] **Marketing push:** full launch marketing, not "beta test" framing. Steam algorithm treats EA launch as a real launch.
- [ ] **Demo / Next Fest:** prepare a self-contained demo (free, controlled). Steam Next Fest visibility is now "the new Early Access."
- [ ] **Store page:** clear expectations — what's playable, what's coming, update cadence. Be honest about state.
- [ ] **Price:** lower than intended 1.0 price. Reward early supporters. Plan gradual increases as content lands.
- [ ] **Trailer:** gameplay-focused, showing the day/night loop and the care fantasy.
- [ ] **Community:** Discord/forum set up before launch; respond to feedback.

## Launch

- [ ] **Scope:** ship a "near-complete" vertical slice (homestead + field + pine camp + rescue), not the full 4-zone chain. Defer 2nd forest/river/marsh/lair to post-EA updates.
- [ ] **Save compatibility:** communicate if updates may break saves (Steam EA docs requirement).
- [ ] **Review strategy:** aim for 1000+ reviews for algorithm blessing. 200 reviews = "valley of sadness."
- [ ] **Discounts:** small launch discount; don't discount further in year 1; use sales for major updates.

## Post-launch

- [ ] **Update cadence:** regular, visible updates. Community expects momentum.
- [ ] **Telemetry live:** session/onboarding telemetry before first public playtest (see TELEMETRY_SPEC.md).
- [ ] **Feedback loop:** manage feedback without getting overwhelmed (see featureupvote strategies).
- [ ] **Scope discipline:** don't overpromise features/timelines. Every promised feature must ship.

## Scope validation (before committing to EA)

- [ ] Phase 1 playtest (onboarding) completed — validates depth-per-encounter bet.
- [ ] Phase 0 (island scale) resolved — validates world sizing.
- [ ] G-ENV at least GREEN on island size and pivot (other blockers can stay red with ruling).

## Notes

- Hollow Knight started as a 3-day game jam prototype that grew into a story-rich platformer — scope can grow, but core must be solid.
- HomeWorld's depth-per-encounter bet (7 axes × one stroke) is the differentiator; validate it before shipping 4 zones.
- "Your EA launch is your launch" — do not treat EA as a beta test or a feedback-gathering exercise.
