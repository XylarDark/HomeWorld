# HomeWorld — theme and long-horizon vision

The prototype's current product direction is in [`Docs/VISION_BOARD.md`](../../Docs/VISION_BOARD.md).
This document holds the game's theme and future-facing campaign direction. It
does not define prototype scope, implementation requirements, engine settings,
or test acceptance.

## Theme

**Love as an epic quest.** The player cares for a home and the people connected
to it, then acts when someone they love is in danger. Care is active: gather
what is needed, ease suffering, help others rest, and bring them home.

The world follows the same moral direction. Encounters aim to heal, calm, or
convert rather than kill. Care is not separate from adventure; it is what the
adventure asks the player to do.

## The day and night rhythm

The game's recurring theme is **gather by day, tend by night**. Day is for
exploration and gathering. At night, the player becomes spirit and tends the
world and those who depend on them. The prototype's current transformation
gates and sequence are defined by the Vision Board and gameplay specification.

## Long-horizon campaign direction

Beyond the prototype, HomeWorld can grow into a journey across worlds shaped by
the Seven Sins and their virtues. Each place gives the player choices between
short-term power that carries a cost and slower care that can restore people
and places. The larger journey continues the work begun at home: face the harm
that divides a world, restore what can be restored, and build a lasting home
with the people who join the journey.

This is a thematic direction, not a locked act structure or content checklist.
Specific campaign length, level count, bosses, succession, multiplayer scope,
and future systems require current product decisions before they guide work.
