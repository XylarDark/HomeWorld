# Slice defaults interview

Open. One lock at a time. Scribe the pick here, then into [`Docs/VIRTUE_ATTRIBUTE_BIBLE.md`](../VIRTUE_ATTRIBUTE_BIBLE.md). Do not start the next round until this one has a pick or a skip.

An axis or a paradox is a layer on a mechanic the creature already has. This interview names the layers for the slice creatures that do not have one yet. It does not add a beast.

## Already locked — do not ask

- The camp ease, the give, the bull, the river jump, the marsh yield, and the snake on the giant already have their layers.
- A normal creature keeps its established mechanic. The layer makes that mechanic unique and harder.
- Which axis appears can be random. The last encounter in a run is a paradox of the stroke.
- The field roost is a climb. One great eagle. One unique nest. It is not a fourth place. The bull knocks out the lower blocker. A skill learned in the pine forest clears the higher obstacle. That skill is not named yet.
- A tree boots you back a few steps. The tree creaks. Spirit form glides you to the bed instead.
- The bench can already place an ease yield on armor and turn it until the rune matches a pole you have drawn.

## Rounds

### Round 1 — The pine-forest skill

The skill that clears the higher roost obstacle.

| Id | Choice |
|----|--------|
| 1 | The stroke, drawn while you climb. The nest is the rune you can see. |
| 2 | The wind-up only. You time the climb. You do not draw on the roost. |
| 3 | Standing still, as on the bull. A sudden move drops you. |
| 4 | The bench. You prepare the mark at home. The climb itself has no stroke. |
| 5 | The skill stays unnamed. The higher obstacle waits. |
| Skip | Leave the skill unnamed. |

Recommend 1. The stroke is the skill the forest has been teaching. The nest is the rune, the way the river stone is the rune.

### Round 2 — How the climb changes the stroke

Ask only if Round 1 was the stroke.

| Id | Choice |
|----|--------|
| 1 | You draw while you climb. A miss drops you to the ledge below. It does not send you home. |
| 2 | You draw only after you reach the nest. The climb itself has no stroke. |
| 3 | The eagle knocks you off. A miss is the weak hit, away from the nest. |
| Skip | Leave the climb's change unnamed. |

Recommend 1. The climb is the default. The stroke happens during it. A drop to the ledge below is the weak hit.

### Round 3 — The tree

The tree already creaks and boots you back a few steps. Spirit form glides you to the bed.

| Id | Choice |
|----|--------|
| 1 | The layer is a stroke on the trunk. The rune is the bark you can see. A miss is the creak and the few steps back. Spirit form still glides you to the bed. |
| 2 | The tree never takes a layer. The creak stays a plain boot. |
| Skip | Leave the tree unnamed. |

Recommend 1. The creak is the default miss. The stroke is the layer.

### Round 4 — Remove, on the bench

Change is the ease-yield recipe. Remove is still unnamed.

| Id | Choice |
|----|--------|
| 1 | Place the armor, turn the rune until it is blank, and commit. The mark comes off in your hand. A commit on the wrong rune fails in the open and leaves the mark. |
| 2 | Remove stays unnamed. The one recipe is enough. |
| Skip | Leave remove unnamed. |

Recommend 1. Place, turn, commit stays the bench. Blank is the turn that removes.
