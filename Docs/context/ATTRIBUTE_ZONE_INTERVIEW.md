# Attribute and zone interview

Open. One lock at a time. Scribe the pick into this file, then into [`Docs/VIRTUE_ATTRIBUTE_BIBLE.md`](../VIRTUE_ATTRIBUTE_BIBLE.md). Do not start the next round until this one has a pick or a skip.

The 2026-10-15 lock already names runes, paradox marks, launch-on-hit, interaction armor, the four places, and the lair's missing layer. This interview does not reopen those. It names the beats that lock left blank.

## Brief

Lead, 2026-10-15. The market hole is the space between Path of Exile's item depth and Nintendo's readable, toy-like play. Sin, virtue, and paradox are the stand-in for that item depth. The planet — its places, encounters, and how they change — is where the play stays simple enough to see. Damage numbers stay out.

## How to run

Each round has competing choices. The recommended option is first. Skip is last. A skip leaves that beat unnamed. After a pick, scribe one sentence into the virtue bible and record the pick here. Then ask the next round.

## Already locked — do not ask

- Seven axes. Pair names stay in `VisionBoard/Core/VISION.md`.
- A paradox mark occupies one axis. The set does not store both poles as two entries.
- Difficulty is the count of sin and virtue runes. A paradox mark changes behaviour and the drop.
- A hit launches you. Armor does not add a damage number.
- The bed rune stays removed. Sleep is the form change.
- Camp complete opens the second forest, then the river of stones, then the marsh. Mountains end exploration.
- Spirit form crosses that river only after the second forest is complete.
- Author the hardest form first, then the scaled-down steps.
- Care stays the encounter. No kill. No health bar.
