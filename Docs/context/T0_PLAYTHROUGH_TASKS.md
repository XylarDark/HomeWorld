## Day

1. Wake on the porch. Partner by the bed. Child by the garden.
2. Interact with `NODE_GARDEN`. One herb.
3. Interact with `NODE_KETTLE`. Tea spends the herb and opens the day sprint. The family hint is "toward the edge."
4. Walk to the perch. Interact. Day glide, camp smoke, no pickup.
5. Land on the field. `NODE_FIELD_GATHER` for herb and seed. `NODE_DUNG` for one dung. `NODE_BULL` to ride. Field only.
6. Interact with `NODE_DAY_CAMP`. The partner is taken, the child waits by the bed, and the day body is sent home.

## Night

7. Interact with `NODE_BED`. Sleep grants the spirit.
8. Night glide from the same perch. Overlap a cloud wisp. Two a night.
9. `NODE_WOUND` spends one wisp. The wound is only seen by day.
10. `NODE_MIX` at the homestead spends the other wisp plus the dung.
11. At camp, interact with each `NODE_GUARD` until three are eased. Then `NODE_CAPTIVE`.
12. `NODE_SHRINE_CAMP` returns home. A bull ride ends in a walk to `NODE_BARN`. The freed partner uses that same return.
