# Boss lair lock

Lead interview, 2026-10-08. Shape only. No meshes. No prove. Not a start read.

Replaces the same-day sleeper-body lock. Vision board still owns the seal, the day-and-night split, and the ban on a new flight mode. This file says how the prototype's last beat uses the loop that already exists.

## The bound giant

One body, many times the player. An Atlas-like sleeper, trapped in a cave. A snake wraps him and locks him in. The body is the place. The snake is the binding, not a second beast to kill.

Four coils, then the head bind. One ram loosens one coil. One guard strike loosens one. The head bind is what he rips off.

## First minute

The same cliff glide. One sign, no pickup, no text. The player steers into the cave mouth. The frame is the mouth, the snake locking the giant.

## What opens the mouth

The cliff glide can show the lair only when the camp rescue loop is already complete: the partner is home, and the three camp guards have been eased once.

The bull ram needs the tamed barn bull. Fertilizer and the homestead wound are not gates for this mouth. That drops two lines from the lock this file replaces.

The ram and the eye stay closed until the giant's wounds and the three guard wounds are healed. No boot. No knockback.

## Day

Heal the giant's wounds. Heal one wound on each of the three guards, the same heal, a different woe from the camp sleep-ease. They accept it. The tamed bull is ridden here only, and a ram loosens one coil. The ride is this lair only. Everywhere else the bull stays field-only.

Contact, not a fight. No health bar. No death.

## Night

Existing shrine portal, or the same glide if you are already spirit. No new flight. The night cap stays two. None are spent on the lair.

The same three guards recognize you. Each strike loosens one coil. They do not kill it.

You soothe the giant's mind. Spirit stealth takes you past the snake's eye, outside its light. At the head you tell him he can wake. He rips off the head bind. The snake is cast off.

## Family

Partner and child stay home. The lair is the player, the three guards, and the giant. No ledge beat. The child is at the bed the seal returns you to.

## What each thing does

| Already done | Job in the lair |
|---|---|
| Bed, sleep | Spirit form. Night entry and the pass by the eye are spirit. |
| Camp night ease | The three guards already know you. The lair help is a second, different woe. |
| Three guards | Heal a wound on each by day. They accept it, then each strike loosens one coil at night. |
| Giant wounds | Day heal. Same verb as the guard wounds. Opens the ram and the eye with the guard heals. |
| Bull, tamed | Ridden on this lair only. One ram loosens one coil. |
| Day glide | The entry. One sign, no pickup, steer into the mouth. |
| Night portal or glide | The return. Existing shrine, or the same glide. No new flight. |
| Spirit stealth | The path past the snake's eye, once the heals are done. |
| Speak at the head | The wake. He rips off the head bind. |
| Partner, child | Stay home. No lair placement. |

## End

The win is a wake, not a kill. The snake is cast off. The rescue is not replayed. The seal sends the player home by the shrine, then they walk to the bed. The bull walks to the barn.

## Not in this lock

New combat, a snake health bar, a snake death, a second mount, a new flight mode, fertilizer as a gate, the homestead wound as a gate, a fail punish.
