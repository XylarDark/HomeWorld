# Boss lair lock

Lead interview, 2026-10-08. Shape only. No meshes. No prove. Not a start read.

The combat bible still owns the seal, the day-and-night split, and the ban on a new flight mode. This file says how the prototype's last beat uses the loop that already exists.

## The sleeper under the cliff

One body, many times the player. It is a suffering sleeper. The body is the place. The first minute is a camera climb up that body, one limb the tea sprint can cross, and no text.

## What opens the mouth

The cliff jump can show the lair only when all of these are already true:

- The partner is home.
- The homestead wound is healed.
- Fertilizer has been spread once.
- The bull is tamed.
- The three camp guards are asleep.

## Entry

The same glide. One sign, no pickup. The player steers into the mouth instead of the field. The fertilizer night flight does not carry them in.

## Day

The tea sprint crosses the tight limb. The bull cannot turn there. The tamed bull walks onto the body, the way it walks to the barn, and is ridden along the long straight of the back. An herb offering keeps the sleeper's threat from filling. Rushing without it boots the player home. The ride is this lair only. Everywhere else the bull stays field-only.

## Night

Three great wounds. Ease each to sleep, the same as a guard. No extra cloud wisp. The night cap stays two. Spirit stealth is how you move between the wounds, outside the sleeper's light. The healed homestead wisps go to the wound you are meant to ease. They do not carry you.

## What each thing does

| Already done | Job in the lair |
|---|---|
| Bed, sleep | Spirit form. Only spirit can ease a wound. |
| Garden herb | The offering that keeps the sleeper from booting you. |
| Tea, day sprint | The tight limb the bull cannot turn onto. |
| Family hint | Once the mouth is open, the one hint points at the lair sign. |
| Partner | Stands at the safe ledge. The hint is theirs. After the seal they walk home with you. |
| Child | Stays home, at the bed the seal returns you to. |
| Day glide | The entry. One sign, no pickup, steer into the mouth. |
| Night glide | The same entry when you are already spirit. Still two wisps. None are spent on the wounds. |
| Homestead wound | The followers mark the great wound to ease. |
| Fertilizer | Spread once on the body before the bull will walk onto it. |
| Shrine | The seal's way home, then the walk to the bed. The bull takes that return and walks to the barn. |
| Three guards | The three wounds, eased to sleep the same way. |
| Bull, tamed | Ridden on the long straight of the back. |
| Bull threat | The sleeper's boot home if you rush without the herb. |
| Spirit stealth | The path between wounds at night. |

## End

Threat uses the existing boot home, and the lair resets. The rescue is not replayed. The seal sends the player home by the shrine, then they walk to the bed. The bull walks to the barn. The win is a seal, not a kill.
