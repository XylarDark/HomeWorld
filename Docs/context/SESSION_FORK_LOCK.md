# Session fork lock

Lead interview, 2026-10-10. Implemented. Replaces the four-card lock of 2026-10-08.

SESSION_START stays the one door. It is the production door. After the clarifier names the work, it points at one craft room. Three rooms: Look, Play, Prove.

Look is art and asset together. The art bible is the law inside Look. It is not a room. Play is beats and locks. Prove is what done means. The agent does not tick sign-off.

A card is short. It names the job, the docs to open, the docs to leave closed, the running list, and the pass bar. Then it stops.

A card points at a settled lock. It follows it. A change to the lock is a new interview, not a sitting in the card.

The clarifier picks the room. It maps the work to one card and names it. You can correct it. No pick means stay in the clarifier. No fourth craft room. Audio and a standalone engineering room stay off this list until a critical-path item is blocked on sound or on code alone.
