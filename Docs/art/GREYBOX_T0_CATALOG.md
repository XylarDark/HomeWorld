# Greybox T0 catalog

The path that crosses Concept into Greybox for a new subject. Greybox is kept when a texture-off mass exists at the written size and the silhouette holds. Polish and a Steam mesh stay closed.

Meters stay in `Docs/art/SCALE.md`. Sheet rules stay in `Docs/art/STILL_GENERATION_STANDARD.md`. The edit steps stay in `Docs/art/COMFY.md`. The size rows stay in `Docs/art/SIZE_TEST_TASKS.md`. This file is the outstanding work and the interviews that block an asset.

## The four steps

1. Design. A new character or asset starts from a design the developer has written into context. If that design is missing, stop. Do not fill it from the measure or from an older asset.
2. Generate. A text image generator makes the first picture from that design only. One subject. Whole meters from the card. Do not attach the measure.
3. Edit. Score the picture with the rating below, then ComfyUI Flux Kontext Dev runs when the score says edit. One change per pass. A Dev output stays out of `Content/`. The measure is the fit check in that score, not a reference image.
4. Mesh. TripoSR makes the basic greybox from the kept front, then the mesh is stood on the locked meter with its origin at the contact. A floor plan and the island pad stay measured blocks, because an image model will not hold those meters.
5. Animate. Characters only. Mixar stores an idle and a walk on that mesh, the same way the player rig was made. Buildings, plants, and props do not get a clip.

A keep or reject sits between steps. A rejected picture is not edited forward and is not meshed. The finish interview, asked only when that picture exists: keep it, or reject it and name the one change.

## Regenerate or edit

Score the picture before the next file. Two gates, in order. The sweet spot is here: a wrong picture is drawn again, and a right picture with one or two local drifts is edited. Three local drifts means the drawing is a different design, so it is drawn again too.

Job gate. All five must pass. Any miss is a regenerate of that view only. Do not edit it.

- The view is the view that was asked. A front is not a three-quarter. A side is not another front.
- The subject is the subject that was asked, and it is the only subject.
- No text, HUD, or rune.
- The picture is still a faceted sheet. It is not a photograph and it is not a second style.
- After a developer design is in context, the picture fits `VisionBoard/KeyArt/homestead_dusk_baseline.jpg`. A smooth blob fails. A crystal-cut outline fails. The measure is not the design source. Locked 2026-10-11.

Drift count. Only if the job gate passed.

- 0. Keep. Do not edit.
- 1 or 2. Edit. Kontext changes the largest drift and is told to keep the rest. A porch roof and its missing posts are one drift, the porch. The second drift waits for the next pass.
- 3 or more. Regenerate. Stacking that many edits drifts the design.
