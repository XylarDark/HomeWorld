# Gap analysis — 2026-10-08 sitting

Archived. The rooms are now Look, Play, and Prove. The phase words are in `Docs/art/PHASES.md`. The canon map points at the live art files. This note is the snapshot from that day.

Against studio practice. Not a playtest. Today's docs are the subject.

## How we are doing

The vision is clear. The art law is locked. The sitting has a track, briefs, a paste block, and four rooms. That is more than most solo projects have before the first mesh. It is not yet a production pack. Studios treat an art bible as a working reference an outsider can follow without asking, with do and don't pictures, a color script per place, and one index that points at every live file. We have the rules in words. We do not have the pictures in the sitting, and the index does not know today's files exist.

## Gaps, in the order they bite a sitting

### 1. The index does not point at today's files

Studios keep one lookup. Our canon map was written for that, and it still misses `VISUAL_NOTES_FOR_GENERATION.md`, `PROTOTYPE_ASSET_TRACK.md`, the four fork cards, and `SESSION_FORK_LOCK.md`. A new chat that starts from the map will not find the sitting. This is the same failure class as the missing stealth pointer.

Fix: five rows in `Docs/CANON_MAP.md` §2. One hour.

### 2. The rune is gone in the vision and still live in the must list

Vision board and art bible say the rune is removed. Canon map §3 still lists must #7 as rune unlock, logic done. Code still has `SetRuneGateUnlocked`. A sitting that follows the map will author a rune. A sitting that follows the bible will not. Studios do not leave two truths.

Fix: mark #7 superseded in the map, and name the bed as the form change. Do not delete the code in this pass. That is a gameplay interview.

### 3. The north stars are not in the sitting

Studios approve against pictures. Our north stars are `.b64` files under `VisionBoard/KeyArt/`. A generator cannot see them. The notes say generate against those and then give words.

Fix: export the three north stars as viewable images next to the notes, or paste a one-line description of each into the notes so the sitting does not depend on a binary.

### 4. The color script is a table, not frames

A production color script is a sequence of small palette frames, one per place. Ours is a temperature table. Enough to stop a grim render. Not enough to stop two sittings inventing two ambers.

Fix: one sentence of value and temperature per place. Do not add hex until a still is accepted.

### 5. No proportion line

Studios write head-to-body and a readable-at distance. We have 1.7–1.8 m and head slightly large. Partner, child, and guards share the face kit with no turnaround.

Fix: one line. Adult head about one sixth of height. Child head larger. Same three face planes.

### 6. Review is not in the engine

Studios review from the player camera, in context. The track says look close and at 20 feet. It does not say screenshot both before the box is checked.

Fix: one line on the asset card. Two screenshots, close and 20 feet, before the box.

### 7. Combat bible is still a locked cousin

The map lists `COMBAT_DREAM_BIBLE.md` as locked. Product direction says care replaces killing. Fork gameplay does not name that bible as closed.

Fix: one line on the gameplay card. Dream combat is not this prototype.

## Do not do

Do not rewrite the art bible into a AAA spec. Polygon budgets and hex palettes before a played cabin are the wrong kind of robust. Do not add a fifth fork. Do not reopen the lair lock to fix a doc gap.

## Order if we close these

1. Canon map rows.
2. Rune marked superseded in the map.
3. North-star lines in the notes.
4. One proportion line.
5. Two-screenshot line on the asset card.
6. Dream-combat line on the gameplay card.

That is the robust version of today. A 10 is still a stranger playing the loop.
