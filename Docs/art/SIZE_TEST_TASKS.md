# Size test tasks

Walk texture-off masses at the locked meters before any polish. The player path is the pattern: one sheet, TripoSR, stand the mesh on the locked meter, origin at the contact, then Mixar. A floor plan and the island are measured blocks. An image model will not hold those meters.

Meters: `Docs/art/SCALE.md`. Sheet rules: `Docs/art/STILL_GENERATION_STANDARD.md`. Room plan: `Docs/context/ART_BIBLE_CONTEXT.md`. Names: `Docs/art/PROTOTYPE_ASSET_BRIEFS.md`.

Drafts stay under `AssetCreation/Exports/` until a pass bar is called true. Do not copy a file into `Content/` from this list. The size-test map is for walking, not for ship.

A size-test mesh may come from one kept front, the same way the player did. That back is a guess. A turnaround is a later sheet, not a blocker for this walk.

## How a row is done

1. One subject. Texture off. Plain ground. Whole meters pasted from the card. The 2 m figure stands beside a creature or a tree. A prop sheet has no person.
2. Keep or reject that sheet before a mesh. One change per reject.
3. TripoSR for one pine, one creature, or one kit piece. Then scale to the row’s meter and put the origin at the contact.
4. Build the cottage and the island pad in Mixar from the meters. Do not send them through TripoSR.
5. One mesh per kind. Pines, sprigs, and rocks are copies. No second tree.
6. When a wave’s rows exist, place that wave in the size-test map and walk it with the player rig.
