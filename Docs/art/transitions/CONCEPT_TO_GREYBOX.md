## Industry

A blockout review checks shape, volume, and proportion against the kept concept. Surface detail and light stay out. A wrong size here is cheap to rebuild. The same correction after materials costs the sheet.

The blockout is the first mass you can measure. A mood still cannot be walked. Build the mass from the kept size pictures. Do not borrow size from the earlier dusk pictures. Those lock light and materials only.

## When generating

Use the kept size picture for that module. Use the dusk pictures only when the prompt asks for light or materials. A greybox prompt does not ask for those.

Words that drifted, and the word to use instead:

| Drift | Say this |
|---|---|
| Human door, round hut, moss dome | Gabled log shell, stone chimney, one player-sized door. |
| Cute green bear, smooth black bear, giant that turns into a bear | The dusk moss-bear. Hard facets. Moss on the upper planes. Amber eyes. |
| Bull prompt that is given the vista | Bull, horns, heavy head. Do not attach the bear picture. |
| Snake laid in an S on the hill | Snake wrapped around the torso. Head by the giant’s head. |
| Player as tall as the door of a cottage | Player is one log tall. The bear and the shell match. |
| Scarf, orb in the hand, skull, white creature on the shoulder | Small shoulder spirit. The scarf belongs to the partner. |
| Photoreal forest, snow, a second tree | One faceted pine, copied. |

Changing tree height in the same prompt as the bear or the cabin drops the facet style or shrinks the bear. One change per pass.
