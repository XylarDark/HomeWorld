## Industry

A concept gate locks design direction before any build view. The review asks whether the silhouette, the proportion, the color, and the material intent fit the game. Detail, texture, lighting, and the 3D form wait.

Studios treat that lock as the cheap place to change the design. A fundamental change after the lock is new work, not a revision. Feedback names this stage and stays inside it. A thumbnail that reads is worth more than a finished rendering of a weak shape.

What this gate reviews:

- Silhouette, proportion, and the feeling of the role.
- Color and material intent, as a mood, not as a surface spec.
- Fit with the pictures already kept.

What stays out until a later transition:

- Shape in 3D, volume, and a measured plan. That review is Concept → Greybox.
- Faceted sheets, front and back as a set. That review is Greybox → Prototype.
- Unwrap, texture, LODs, export. That review is Polish → Final.

A mood still is not a playtest. The blockout is the first thing you can walk. That line is already in `Docs/34_ART_PIPELINE_RESEARCH.md`. The in-engine ladder in `Docs/37_POLISH_PASS_PROCESS.md` starts at S1 blockout, which is the next transition.

Sources: [Saigon Dragon, concept to final](https://saigondragonstudios.com/2026/06/23/game-art-pipeline-concept-to-final-asset/), [Game Art Services, concept pipeline](https://gameartservices.com/blog/concept-art-pipeline-guide), [Saigon Dragon, feedback scoped to the stage](https://saigondragonstudios.com/2026/06/18/art-feedback-process-game-art-revision-rounds/), [Nasty Rodent, concept lock before 3D](https://nastyrodent.com/quality-control-for-outsourced-game-art-building-gates-that-actually-catch-problems/).
